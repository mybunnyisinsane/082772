Latin Forms Quizzer v7 — All-section audit

Problem found
- The quizzer limited Questions to max 10.
- Every course/topic was subdivided into fixed chunks of 10, with no whole-section option.
- Therefore any section/list longer than 10 could not be tested as a complete section.

Latin III/IV affected sections
- Historia Celata: 27 words
- Mostellaria: 30 words
- Hannibal: 42 words
- Catullus: 24 words
- Sallust: 64 words
All five were affected.

Other affected quizzer areas
- Latin I units longer than 10 words
- Latin II inherited Latin I units longer than 10 words
- Latin II extension topics longer than 10 words
- AP Core/Mastery lists longer than 10 words

Fix
- Added "All words in section (N)" for Latin I/II/III-IV.
- Added "All Core words (N)" / "All Mastery words (N)" for AP.
- Preserved 10-word set options.
- Questions maximum now dynamically matches the selected pool size instead of being capped at 10.
- Fixed Latin III/IV topic order to Historia Celata, Mostellaria, Hannibal, Catullus, Sallust.
- Default remains Set 1 so the established 10-word workflow is unchanged unless "All" is chosen.

Validation
- JavaScript syntax checked with node --check.
