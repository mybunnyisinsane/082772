New distinction

Dropped sounds and letters

Poetic contraction shortens a word’s written form:

vincula→vincla

Elision happens between words in recitation: a final vowel (or vowel + m) is not fully sounded before a following vowel or h.

Both can make familiar vocabulary look or sound unfamiliar—but they are not the same process.
