Prototype rules

Latin answers ignore capitalization and macrons. English answers ignore capitalization, punctuation, and a leading to for verbs. Source-attested synonyms and separate contextual senses are accepted. Master Practice is the default tier; Core and Full Reading Vocabulary remain selectable by author. It generates common indicative forms for regular verbs and common case/number forms for regular nouns and adjectives; irregular or deponent entries use safe listed forms.
